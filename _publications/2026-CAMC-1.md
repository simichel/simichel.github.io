Abstract
=========
We present a high-order nodal discontinuous Galerkin method for nonlinear hyperbolic systems on fully unstructured three-dimensional polyhedral meshes. The approach extends agglomerated finite element basis functions to polyhedral control volumes by representing the solution with piecewise continuous polynomials on an internal tetrahedral subgrid. This construction allows standard reference-tetrahedron finite element technology to be reused inside each polyhedral cell, including precomputed mass and stiffness matrices. High-order time accuracy is obtained with a local space-time Galerkin predictor in the ADER framework. An artificial viscosity limiter is included to stabilize shocks while preserving accuracy in smooth regions. The method is assessed on three-dimensional benchmark problems for the compressible Euler and Navier-Stokes equations.
