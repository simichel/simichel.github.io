Abstract
=========
We present a high-order nodal discontinuous Galerkin method for nonlinear hyperbolic systems of PDEs on fully unstructured three-dimensional polyhedral meshes. The method extends agglomerated finite element basis functions to polyhedral grids: inside each polyhedral cell, the discrete solution is represented by continuous piecewise polynomials defined on an internal tetrahedral subgrid.

This construction makes it possible to reuse standard finite element technology on reference tetrahedra, including precomputed mass, stiffness, and flux matrices, while keeping the flexibility of arbitrary polyhedral meshes. High-order accuracy in time is obtained with a local space-time Galerkin predictor in the ADER framework. To handle shocks and steep gradients, an artificial viscosity strategy is included in the scheme.

The approach is designed for nonlinear hyperbolic systems, with applications to the three-dimensional compressible Euler and Navier-Stokes equations. Numerical benchmarks are used to assess accuracy, robustness, and shock-capturing capabilities on unstructured polyhedral meshes.

**Keywords:** high-order ADER-DG schemes, agglomerated finite element basis functions, unstructured polyhedral meshes, Euler and Navier-Stokes equations.
